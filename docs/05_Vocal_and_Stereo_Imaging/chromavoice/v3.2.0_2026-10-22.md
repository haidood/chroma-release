## WARP Granular OLA Transient Preservation & Consonant Micro-Windowing

### What Changed & Why It Matters Musically

- **WARP Granular OLA Engine**: Fast differential envelope follower preserves vocal consonants and plosives.
- **Zero-Transcendental C1 Polynomial Hann Window**: Ultra-low CPU overhead even when stacking multiple vocal harmony instances.

---
### ⚡ How to Update in 10 Seconds
1. Click the **`[ v2.x / CHECK ]`** button in the top-right header bar of the plugin.
2. Close your DAW and click **`[ YES ]`** in the prompt to run `ChromaUpdater.exe`.
3. Re-open your DAW — you are now on the latest build!
