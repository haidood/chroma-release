# ChromaPulse `v2.8.0` — Release Notes (2026-10-21)

**[⬅ Back to ChromaPulse Overview & Glossary](./README.md)**

## 7-Zone Acoustic EQ Backdrop, Stack-Allocated Spectrum & 0.002f Fine Stepping

### What Changed & Why It Matters Musically

- **7-Zone Studio Mixing Backdrop**: Visual frequency guide (Sub, Bass, Low-Mid, Mid, Upper-Mid, Presence, Air).
- **Zero-Lag Spectrum & EQ Rendering**: Stack-allocated `DrawLines` curve rendering with cut-filter vertical `Q` dragging.
- **Universal Auto-Updater Shield**: 1-click in-plugin updates.

---
### ⚡ How to Update in 10 Seconds
1. Click the **`[ v2.x / CHECK ]`** button in the top-right header bar of the plugin.
2. Close your DAW and click **`[ YES ]`** in the prompt to run `ChromaUpdater.exe`.
3. Re-open your DAW — you are now on the latest build!
