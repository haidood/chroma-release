# ChromaCore VST `v2.8.0` — Release Notes (2026-10-10)

## 12-Band Studio EQ, Persistent Double-Buffering & Zero-CPU Idle Throttling

### What Changed & Why It Matters Musically

- **12-Band Dynamic Studio EQ**: Full parametric EQ with 7 acoustic mixing zone backdrops, mousewheel `Q` control, and real-time spectrum analyzer.
- **Zero-Allocation 60 FPS GUI**: Persistent GDI+ backbuffer cache and intelligent idle repaint throttling (`0.0%` CPU when stopped in Ableton Live / FL Studio).
- **Universal Update Shield & Crash Vault**: Built-in 1-click updater and automatic session crash recovery.

---
### ⚡ How to Update in 10 Seconds
1. Click the **`[ v2.8.x / CHECK ]`** button in the top-right header bar of the plugin.
2. Close your DAW and click **`[ YES ]`** in the prompt to run `ChromaUpdater.exe`.
3. Re-open Ableton Live / FL Studio — you are now on the latest build!
