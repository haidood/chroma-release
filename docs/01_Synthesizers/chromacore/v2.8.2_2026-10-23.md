# ChromaCore & ChromaCorePro `v2.8.2` — Release Notes (2026-10-23)

**[⬅ Back to ChromaCore & ChromaCorePro Overview & Glossary](./README.md)**

## 8-Style Universal Modulation Oscillator Overhaul & Dual Filter Routing Fix

### What Changed & Why It Matters Musically

- **8 Modulation Oscillator Styles**: Upgraded the `MOD OSC // SHAPER & FM` deck with 8 distinct synthesis modes: *RingMod*, *PhaseFM*, *WTMorph*, *ExpoFM*, *HardSync*, *Wavefolder*, *DriveScream*, and *Tremolo*.
- **10 Modulation Targets**: Route the Mod Osc to *Osc 1*, *Osc 2*, *Osc 1+2*, *Osc 3*, *Filter 1*, *Filter 2*, *Filter 1+2*, *Volume*, *Pan*, or *Pitch*.
- **Filter 2 Parallel Routing Fix**: Fixed a bug where oscillators routed to Filter 2 (`F2`) were attenuated when `FilterMix` was at default.

### ⚡ How to Update in 10 Seconds
1. Click the **`[ v2.8.x / CHECK ]`** button in the top-right header bar of the plugin.
2. Close your DAW and click **`[ YES ]`** in the prompt to run `ChromaUpdater.exe`.
3. Re-open Ableton Live / FL Studio — you are now on the latest build!
