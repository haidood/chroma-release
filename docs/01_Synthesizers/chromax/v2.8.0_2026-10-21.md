# ChromaX `v2.8.0` — Release Notes (2026-10-21)

**[⬅ Back to ChromaX Overview & Glossary](./README.md)**

## Zero Background Noise on Clean Presets, Polyphonic Headroom Scaling & Dirty-Rect UI

### What Changed & Why It Matters Musically

- **Silent Noise Floor on Clean Patches**: Defaulted `kParamNoise1Level = 0.0f` on clean factory presets so pure keys and pads have zero background hiss.
- **Polyphonic Headroom Scaling**: Smooth multi-voice gain compensation prevents chord clipping.
- **60 FPS Dirty-Rect Invalidation & Shift Fine-Tune**: `0.002f` Shift-drag precision and Universal Auto-Updater Shield.

---
### ⚡ How to Update in 10 Seconds
1. Click the **`[ v2.8.x / CHECK ]`** button in the top-right header bar of the plugin.
2. Close your DAW and click **`[ YES ]`** in the prompt to run `ChromaUpdater.exe`.
3. Re-open Ableton Live / FL Studio — you are now on the latest build!
