# ChromaDeep `v2.8.0` — Release Notes (2026-10-21)

**[⬅ Back to ChromaDeep Overview & Glossary](./README.md)**

## 7-Zone Mixing Backdrop, Highpass/Lowpass Vertical Q Drag & Auto-Updater Shield

### What Changed & Why It Matters Musically

- **7 Acoustic Mixing Zones**: Full parametric EQ with vertical `Q` resonance dragging on Highpass and Lowpass filters.
- **Zero-Allocation 60 FPS Paint Loop**: Eliminated GDI heap allocations for zero DAW lag.

---
### ⚡ How to Update in 10 Seconds
1. Click the **`[ v2.x / CHECK ]`** button in the top-right header bar of the plugin.
2. Close your DAW and click **`[ YES ]`** in the prompt to run `ChromaUpdater.exe`.
3. Re-open your DAW — you are now on the latest build!
