# ChromaDelay `v2.8.0` — Release Notes (2026-10-21)

**[⬅ Back to ChromaDelay Overview & Glossary](./README.md)**

## Flat 0.0 dB Default EQ Bands, 7 Mixing Zones & Zero Heap Allocations

### What Changed & Why It Matters Musically

- **5 Authentic Delay Character Models**: BBD Analog, 15 IPS Studio Tape, Tel-Ray Oil-Can, 12-Bit Vintage PCM, and Shimmer Pitch Cascade.
- **Interactive Feedback EQ & Ducking**: Built-in sidechain ducking keeps echoes out of the way while your lead vocal or synth is playing, then blooms when the phrase stops.

---
### ⚡ How to Update in 10 Seconds
1. Click the **`[ v2.8.x / CHECK ]`** button in the top-right header bar of the plugin.
2. Close your DAW and click **`[ YES ]`** in the prompt to run `ChromaUpdater.exe`.
3. Re-open Ableton Live / FL Studio — you are now on the latest build!
